Common Questions

How do I book a car with Pingle Ride?

Simply call or WhatsApp us at 085216 67890, or use the booking form on this page. We'll confirm your booking within minutes.

Is there a minimum booking duration?

For local city rides, bookings start at 4 hours. For outstation trips, there's no minimum — one-way rides are available.

Are your prices inclusive of fuel?

Yes! All our fares include fuel, driver charges, and tolls. The rate you're quoted is the rate you pay — no surprises at the end.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, free cancellation is available up to 1 hour before the scheduled pickup. Just call us and we'll handle it immediately.

Do you offer corporate or bulk booking discounts?

Absolutely. We have special packages for corporate clients, wedding bookings, and group travel. Contact us to discuss a custom plan.

Which cities can you travel to from Ranchi?

We cover all major destinations from Ranchi including Jamshedpur, Dhanbad, Hazaribagh, Bokaro, Patna, Kolkata, and more. Call us for a custom quote.
